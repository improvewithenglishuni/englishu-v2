# SMALLPOX Reel: sources

## Facts used

| Claim (scene) | Source |
|---|---|
| Smallpox is the only human disease eradicated (s01) | WHO smallpox fact page: https://www.who.int/health-topics/smallpox |
| Caused by the variola virus; spread person to person (s02) | WHO: https://www.who.int/health-topics/smallpox |
| Eradicated through vaccination plus surveillance after WHO's intensified plan in 1967 (s03, counter starts at 1967) | WHO: https://www.who.int/emergencies/situations/smallpox |
| Declared eradicated by the 33rd World Health Assembly, 8 May 1980 (s04) | UN press release: https://press.un.org/en/1996/19960125.h2894.html ; WHO: https://www.who.int/health-topics/smallpox |
| Last natural case: Somalia, October 1977 (s04, the circled dot) | UN press release above; Guinness World Records: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/67647-rarest-disease |

Scene 3 visualizes the vaccination idea (vaccinating everyone around a case
stops the chain), not specific campaign numbers.

## Images

All illustrations (virus, people, syringe, globe) are drawn in code for this
Reel; no third-party images. Archival photos were not used because the
render environment cannot reach Wikimedia Commons.
