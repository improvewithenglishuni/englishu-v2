## Decisions in v7
- **DISPARITY replaces DISCREPANCY.** Discrepancy is a mismatch between two things that should agree (two reports, two
  figures). Prices and wages growing at different rates is a gap or inequality: disparity. DISPARITY is in the Master
  Word Bank (Nouns, Advanced, פער, שוני).
- **Hebrew glosses are back on screen** (פער, מופקע), never spoken: series rule.
- **Base year stays 1995** (said on screen: "1995 = 100"); 1995-2007 is fast-forwarded in 2 s, so the hook is not
  contradicted for long, and line 2 says it out loud.
- **EXORBITANT is acted out**: the tag carries a real price (CBS average 2025), rides the red line, then grows to the
  centre as the word is written on it. The "14.3 years of salary" figure gives it weight (CBS Table 2.2 2025 average
  price ÷ 12 × CBS 2025 average wage).
- **Hebrew in a clean font (Rubik)** for the hook, labels, glosses and the share note; Latin stays in the handwriting
  font; EXORBITANT in Permanent Marker.
- **Title**: "Apartment Prices vs. Salaries", larger, below Instagram's top bar (y 250); sub-title "Israel · 1995 = 100 ·
  CBS data". EXORBITANT stays out of the title because the chart measures growth, not price (see v4 title check).
- **Safe area**: tip labels end by x ≈ 925; nothing below y 1480 except the share note.
- **Sound**: library rules (two whooshes, thumps only on the key reveals, room tone, nothing over the end card).
