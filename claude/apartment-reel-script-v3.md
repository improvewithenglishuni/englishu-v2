# Apartment-price Reel: script v3 (mock-up v2, chart-first style)

Earlier versions are kept: `apartment-reel-script.md` (v1) and `apartment-reel-script-v2.md`.
v3 follows Sharon's Nov 28 notes: the style of the "How Do Couples Meet?" Reel, one continuous chart, less wording,
slower, y-axis on the left (so time runs left to right), and the gap explained only at the end. DISCREPANCY added as a second word.

Target words: **EXORBITANT** (word bank: Advanced) and **DISCREPANCY** (not in the Master Word Bank; it appears only as a synonym of DISPARITY, which is Advanced). Both are taught from context, with no Hebrew gloss on screen (Sharon's request).

## Recording script (= subtitle text)
1. כמה שנות משכורת עולה היום דירה ממוצעת בישראל?  *(curious; pause)*
2. בואו נתחיל ב-2011.  *(light, then silence while the chart runs)*
3. ואז המחירים זינקו.  *(said over 2021-2022, when the red line jumps)*
4. המשכורת עלתה ב-58 אחוז, ומחיר הדירה יותר מהכפיל את עצמו.  *(steady, stress the second half)*
5. That's the discrepancy. Today, an average apartment costs an exorbitant fourteen years of salary.  *(stress both words)*
6. חוסכים לדירה ומרגישים שאתם לא מתקרבים? זה לא אתם.  *(warm, smile)*

About 45 words. Story 32.0 s + end card 2.7 s = 34.7 s.

## Scene (one continuous shot, no cuts)
- Whole Reel: the grid page, title "The EXORBITANT Price of an Apartment" at the top, a hand-drawn year slider under it, the chart below.
- 0-4 s: empty axes, slider at 2011. Hook line.
- 4-20 s: the two lines grow left to right (about 1.1 s per year) and the time axis stretches so the tips stay at the right, as in the reference. Live labels at the tips: "מחיר דירה +X%" (red) and "שכר ממוצע +X%" (blue). A highlighter bar between the two tips tracks the gap all the way.
- 20-24 s: lines finished at 2025 (+108% and +58%).
- 24 s: "DISCREPANCY" is written above the lines, with an arrow to the bar.
- 26.3 s: highlighter swipe over EXORBITANT in the title. 27 s: "2025: דירה ממוצעת = 14.3 שנות שכר ממוצע" written under the chart.
- 29-32 s: hold, then end card.

## Data
As in v2 (CBS Dwellings Price Index, Table 2.1; CBS wage per employee job, series 615908; CBS Table 2.2 for 14.3 years). Logged in `tools/sources/SOURCES.md`.
"ואז המחירים זינקו" matches the data: the index rose from 159.8 (2021) to 187.4 (2022), the largest one-year rise in the series.

## Departures from the skill rules (by Sharon's direction for this Reel)
- One continuous chart instead of push and pull with photos and cuts.
- Live values at the line tips the whole time (as in the reference), not only while narrated.
- No Hebrew gloss; the words are taught by context.
