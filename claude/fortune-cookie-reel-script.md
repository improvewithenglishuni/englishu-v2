## Accuracy notes
- 1878 print: *Moshiogusa Kinsei Kidan* (1878) shows a man baking tsujiura senbei; found by Yasuko Nakamachi (Jennifer 8. Lee, *The Fortune Cookie Chronicles*, 2008; NYT, 16 Jan 2008).
- Makoto Hagiwara served them at the Japanese Tea Garden, Golden Gate Park (1890s–1910s); his press (c. 1914) is the one photographed. Other early Japanese-American bakers in California are also credited, so "one of the first" (line 7) is accurate.
- WWII: Japanese Americans were incarcerated from 1942, their bakeries closed, and Chinese-American manufacturers took over production. Line 9 now states this without "כנראה"; it is the account most historians give (Jennifer 8. Lee; Yasuko Nakamachi), though not documented in detail.
- The Lange photo is a grocery, not a bakery; it carries only the place/date banner, so it does not claim to be a bakery.
- Amirnet exemption: 134 and above.
