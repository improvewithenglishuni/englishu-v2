## Open: starting in the 1970s
Not possible from official, consistent data in hand:
- The CBS Dwellings Price Index (Table 2.1) starts in 1994 (base 1993 = 100).
- CBS average wage per employee job: the 2011-classification series starts in 2011; before that the series comes in
  separate pieces (1995-2001, 2002-2004, 2004-2012) with processing and classification changes.
- 1970s-1985 Israel had very high inflation, so "growth since the 1970s" in % would be in the thousands for both lines.
Options: (A) keep 2011-2025 (clean); (B) start in the mid-1990s by linking the CBS wage pieces at their overlaps (a
documented splice, flagged on screen); (C) a long-run ratio ("years of salary for one apartment") from a Bank of Israel
or CBS long-term publication, if one exists back to the 1970s; Sharon would need to find or approve the source.
