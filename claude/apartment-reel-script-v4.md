# Apartment-price Reel: script v4 (mock-up v3)

Earlier versions are kept (v1, v2, v3). Changes from Sharon's Nov 28 notes:
- The "saving for an apartment and never getting there" line is now the hook, on a real Tel Aviv building with a swinging
  EXORBITANT PRICES tag, then a hard cut to the chart at 3.2 s.
- Much less wording at the end: the visuals speak.
- New title (see "Title check" below).

Target words: EXORBITANT (word bank: Advanced) and DISCREPANCY (not in the bank; a synonym of DISPARITY, Advanced). Taught by context, no gloss.

## Recording script (= subtitle text)
1. חוסכים לדירה ומרגישים שאתם אף פעם לא מגיעים?  *(hook, warm and knowing; pause)*
2. Apartment prices in Israel have become exorbitant.  *(stress "exorbitant")*
3. בואו נשווה אותם למשכורות, מאז 2011.  *(light; then silence while the chart runs)*
4. ואז המחירים זינקו.  *(over 2021-2022, the biggest one-year rise in the data)*
5. That's the discrepancy.  *(slow, let it land; then silence)*

About 25 words. Story 29.3 s + end card 4.2 s = 33.5 s.

## Title check
The v2 title, "The EXORBITANT Price of an Apartment", did not describe the chart. The chart does not show the price of an
apartment: it shows how much prices and salaries have each grown since 2011 (both indexed to 2011 = 100). Its story is the
gap between two growth rates, not a price level. "Exorbitant" is also a judgment, not a measurement. New title:
**"Apartment Prices vs. Salaries"**, with the small line **"Israel · growth since 2011"**, which says exactly what is plotted.
EXORBITANT moves to the hook tag and the spoken line, where it is clearly the narrator's description.
