## 3. Accuracy notes

**Chart choice (my recommendation): two lines indexed to 100 in the same base year.** Both series come from one official source (the Central Bureau of Statistics), and both are nominal, so they can be compared directly. The "years of salary" number in lines 2 and 6 is then just the ratio of the same two CBS figures, so it needs no third source:
`years = CBS average price of a dwelling (annual) ÷ (12 × CBS average monthly wage per employee job, same year)`.
A ready-made "number of salaries" series exists, but it is published by the Ministry of Construction and Housing and is mostly quoted through the press. I would only use it if Sharon can download it from the ministry itself.

**Tables to download (please attach as CSV or XLSX, annual values, every year available up to 2025):**
1. **Apartment prices:** CBS, Series DataBank (מאגר הסדרות העתיות) > Prices (מחירים) > Dwellings (מחירי דירות):
   - (a) **Dwellings price index** (מדד מחירי הדירות), annual average, the linked series (one continuous series from the earliest year), and
   - (b) **Average price of a dwelling**, all of Israel (מחיר ממוצע של דירה בבעלות הדיירים, סך הכל), annual average, NIS thousand. The same figure is in Price Statistics Monthly, Table 2.2 "Average Prices of Dwellings".
2. **Salary:** CBS, Series DataBank > Labour and wages (שכר ותעסוקה) > **Average monthly wage per employee job, total economy, nominal** (שכר ממוצע למשרת שכיר, כלל המשק, במחירים שוטפים), annual average, NIS, from the same earliest year to 2025.

Before using them I will:
- check every footnote for a series break or definition change (for example a new methodology for the dwellings index, or whether the wage series includes workers from abroad) and pick a base year after any break;
- use the price index for the two chart lines (it is quality-adjusted), and the average price only for the "years of salary" number;
- show end values rounded to whole percent, with the rounding stated in these notes.

**Conditions on the wording, to check against the data:**
- Line 3 ("וזה לא תמיד היה ככה") and line 6 are true only if [M] is clearly smaller than [N]. If the data does not show that, the lines change.
- Line 5 says only that prices rose more than wages. No cause is given anywhere in the narration (no claim about interest rates, supply or demand).
- The base year [START] must be stated on screen ("[START] = 100") and chosen for a data reason (the earliest clean year after any break), not to make the gap look bigger. 2025 is the last full year; if the 2025 annual average is still provisional, the chart says so in a small handwritten note, or ends in 2024.
- Line 6 compares the same metric at two points, both from CBS.
- The y-axis is drawn to scale. Where the price line leaves the top of the page, its true end value is written at the exit point, so the tear is a metaphor and not a cut-off axis.
- The "for sale" photo must show no real agency name or phone number.

**Sources logged** in `tools/sources/SOURCES.md` (access date 2026-11-26).
