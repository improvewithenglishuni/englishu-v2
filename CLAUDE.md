# EnglishU Reels

## Skills
Always use the `englishu-reel` and `vox-motion-graphics` skills together for every Reel. They are complementary, not alternatives:
`vox-motion-graphics` holds the Vox look and how to build it; `englishu-reel` holds the series rules. Where a specific rule conflicts, the `englishu-reel` rule applies
(no credits without Sharon's approval, her voice only, no text inside AI clips).

## Sound effects
A ready sound-effects library is in `public/sfx/` (six folders: mechanical, tech, tactile, ambience, whooshes, miscellaneous).
Read `public/sfx/README.md` before adding sound to any Reel: it has the mix levels, the rules (variation, at most two soft whooshes,
J-cut, restraint) and a Remotion example. `public/sfx/manifest.json` lists every file and its variations.
Use it for every Reel unless Sharon says otherwise.
